OUTPUT:
+---------+------------+------------+-------------+
| OrderID | CustomerID | OrderDate  | TotalAmount |
+---------+------------+------------+-------------+
|     101 |          1 | 2026-09-20 |     1500.00 |
|     102 |          2 | 2026-09-18 |     2500.00 |
|     103 |          3 | 2026-09-15 |     1800.00 |
|     104 |          4 | 2026-09-10 |     3500.00 |
+---------+------------+------------+-------------+
REASON:

The displayed orders have dates within the last 30 days of the assumed current date (2026-09-30). Order 105 is not shown because its date is 2026-08-20, which is before the cutoff of 2026-08-31.

QUESTION 6:
Retrieve the highest, lowest, and average order amount using
aggregate functions.
------------------------------------------------------------

QUERY:
SELECT
MAX(TotalAmount) AS HighestAmount,
MIN(TotalAmount) AS LowestAmount,
AVG(TotalAmount) AS AverageAmount
FROM Orders;

OUTPUT:

+---------------+--------------+---------------+
| HighestAmount | LowestAmount | AverageAmount |
+---------------+--------------+---------------+
|       3500.00 |      1500.00 |   2325.000000 |
+---------------+--------------+---------------+
1 row in set (0.032 sec)REASON:

The displayed Orders table contains amounts of 1500.00, 2500.00, 1800.00, and 3500.00. Their sum is 9300.00, and the average is 9300.00 / 4 = 2325.00. MAX() returns 3500.00 and MIN() returns 1500.00.

QUESTION 5:
Count how many times a specific product has been sold using COUNT().
------------------------------------------------------------

QUERY:
SELECT
    ProductID,
    COUNT(*) AS NumberOfOrders
FROM OrderDetails
WHERE ProductID = 202
GROUP BY ProductID;

OUTPUT:

+-----------+----------------+
| ProductID | NumberOfOrders |
+-----------+----------------+
|       202 |              2 |
+-----------+----------------+

REASON:

The query filters for ProductID = 202. In the inserted OrderDetails table, ProductID 202 appears in OrderDetailID 301 and 304, so COUNT(*) = 2. Here, “times sold” means the number of order-detail records. The total quantity for ProductID 202 is 1 + 1 = 2 units.
